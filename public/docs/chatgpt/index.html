English Punch for ChatGPT

The English Punch connector lets ChatGPT organize vocabulary cards and run spaced-repetition reviews after you sign in and grant permission. ChatGPT never needs your English Punch password or a private API key.

Permissions

  • profile:read reads your basic account profile.
  • bags:read and bags:write manage bags.
  • cards:read and cards:write manage cards.
  • reviews:read and reviews:write run and inspect reviews.

Safety

The connector derives your identity from a short-lived OAuth access token. Delete operations and card replacements are marked destructive; replacing a card resets its FSRS schedule. Review response time is measured by English Punch rather than supplied by ChatGPT.

Disconnect

Remove or disconnect English Punch from the ChatGPT Plugins page to stop future access. You can also revoke the authorization from your English Punch account when account-level connection management is available.
